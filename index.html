Everything happens in the viewfinder

Filters are not a post‑processing step. RasterLab renders the shader into the live preview and writes that same filtered output to disk while you record.

Catalog

A shader library, not a filter pack

Retro gaming consoles and handhelds, arcade CRTs, home video, cinema grades, monitor emulations and artistic effects. Multi‑pass presets such as CRT‑Royale are supported.

Recording

Filtered video and Live Photos

Record with the look applied, with audio, straight to your Photo Library. Live Photos keep the filtered motion. Recordings are written as recoverable fragments so an interruption does not lose the take.

Gestures

Full‑screen controls

Swipe left for a random shader, swipe right to step back through your history, pinch to zoom, double‑tap to focus, long‑press to lock focus and tap once to hide the UI.

Editing

Trim and review clips

Open your recordings in the built‑in editor to scrub the filmstrip, trim the ends and keep the parts you want before sharing.

System integration

Lock Screen, Control Center, Siri

Launch capture from the Lock Screen camera control, Control Center and Home Screen widgets, Siri and Shortcuts. Edit existing photos through the Photos editing extension, and share into RasterLab from other apps.

Apple Watch

Remote shutter on your wrist

The RasterLab Remote watch app talks to your iPhone over Watch Connectivity so you can start and stop capture from a distance.

Built for the GPU you already have

RasterLab's rendering pipeline is written in Swift 6 and Metal. Camera delivery keeps only the newest frame, preview and recording run on their own workers, and the shader catalog ships with hash‑verified thumbnails so the browser never has to compile a shader just to show you a preview.

  • Quality presets at 30, 60 and 120 frames per second, switchable without restarting capture.
  • Pre‑roll recording keeps a bounded buffer of compressed video so you can capture a moment that already happened.
  • Recovery journal restores an interrupted recording and lets you save, restore or discard it explicitly.
  • Optional location tagging of captures, only when you grant location access.

Requirements

iPhone and iPad

iOS 26.0 or later. A device with a Metal‑capable GPU is required for real‑time shaders. ProMotion displays unlock the 120 Hz preset.

Apple Watch (optional)

watchOS 10.6 or later for the RasterLab Remote companion app.

Permissions

Camera, Microphone and Photo Library are needed to capture and save. Location and Siri are optional. See the privacy policy for details.
