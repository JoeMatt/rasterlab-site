RasterLab Privacy Policy

RasterLab is a camera app. Your photos and videos are rendered and stored on your device. This policy explains which device features the app uses, what it stores, and what it does not do.

Applies to: RasterLab for iOS and iPadOS, its app extensions, and the RasterLab Remote watch app · Developer: Matti Media LLC · Effective date: to be set on publication

Summary

  • RasterLab's App Store privacy manifest declares no collected data types and no tracking.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash‑reporting SDKs, and no code that uploads media or usage information to a server.
  • Camera, microphone and photo library access are used only to capture, render and save your media on the device.
  • Location access is optional and, when granted, is used to tag your captures.
  • Settings and in‑progress recordings are stored locally, including in a shared container used by the app's extensions.

Data collection and tracking

RasterLab ships an Apple privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) in the app and in its shared framework. Both declare:

TrackingNSPrivacyTracking = false. The app does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.
Tracking domainsNone.
Collected data typesNone. The app does not send personal data, identifiers, usage data or diagnostics to the developer.

Apple may collect crash logs and analytics from your device according to your own iOS settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements). That data is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not by RasterLab.

Device permissions

iOS asks for each permission the first time it is needed and shows the purpose string below. You can change any of them later in Settings.

The Lock Screen capture extension requests Camera and Photo Library permissions with equivalent purpose strings.

What is stored on your device

  • Captured media. Finished photos, Live Photos and videos are saved to your Photo Library. The developer has no access to them.
  • Recordings in progress and recovery data. While recording, RasterLab writes recoverable movie fragments and a small recovery journal to local app storage. If a recording is interrupted, these are kept until you choose Save, Restore or Discard. Live still captures are held in a durable local inbox until they are imported into Photos.
  • Preferences. Filter favorites, saved filter chains, quality preset, recent filter history and similar settings are stored with UserDefaults.
  • Shared container. The app and its extensions (Lock Screen capture, widgets, Share, Photos editing and Spotlight) share an App Group container (group.com.mattimedia.rasterlab) so they can read the same settings and hand off captures. This container is on your device only.
  • Spotlight index. The Spotlight extension can index items so they appear in on‑device search. Spotlight indexes stay on the device.
  • No iCloud sync by the app. RasterLab does not use iCloud or CloudKit entitlements. If your Photo Library syncs through iCloud Photos, that is your Apple account setting and is governed by Apple.

Deleting the app removes its local storage and shared container. Media already saved to Photos remains in your library.

Sharing and third parties

RasterLab does not sell, rent or share personal data with third parties. Media leaves your device only when you choose to share it using the iOS Share sheet or when your Photo Library syncs under your own Apple account settings.

The app includes the following open‑source components. They run entirely on your device and do not communicate with their authors:

  • Zip — archive handling for bundled shader resources.
  • Alloy — Metal rendering helpers.

The RasterLab Remote watch app exchanges control messages with the iPhone app over Apple's Watch Connectivity framework between your own paired devices.

Network access

Our review of the app's source found no networking code: no URL sessions, sockets or background transfer tasks, and no third‑party analytics, advertising or crash‑reporting libraries. The app does not need an internet connection to function.

Children

Needs owner confirmation: RasterLab is a general‑audience camera app and is not directed at children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it collects no personal information at all.

Your choices

  • Revoke or change any permission at any time in Settings → Apps → RasterLab. The app continues to work without optional permissions such as Location and Siri.
  • Delete captures from the Photos app. Delete the app to remove all local app data.
  • Because RasterLab holds no account or server‑side data about you, there is nothing for the developer to export, correct or delete on request. Owner to confirm applicable regional rights language (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA) if required for the markets where the app is sold.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of RasterLab adds a feature that changes how data is handled, this page will be updated and the effective date revised before that version is released.
